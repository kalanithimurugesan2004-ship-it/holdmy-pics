Place your logo and "thanks card" files in this folder so the site can reference them at runtime.

Product image override filenames (place these to replace product cards):

- magazines-photobooks.jpg   -> replaces the "Magazines & Photobooks" product image
- polaroids.jpg              -> replaces the "Polaroids" product image
- posters.jpg                -> replaces the "Posters" product image

After copying files here, restart the dev server:
